---
title: "Bookmarks"
description: "How to add bookmarks to a prose reader: annotations without a range, which the annotations enhancer gives by page."
---

Bookmarks are annotations without a text selection: their CFI has no range. The [annotations enhancer](/enhancers/annotations#bookmarks-and-highlights) gives them the kind `"bookmark"`, and gives the bookmarks of each page with [`bookmarksByItemPage$`](/enhancers/annotations#finding-the-bookmarks-of-a-page). What else a bookmark holds, such as a note, is up to your app. Visit the [Annotations](/learn/annotations) section to learn more.
