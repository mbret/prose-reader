---
title: "Bookmarks"
description: "How to add bookmarks to a prose reader: build each one on the annotation model, and decide in your app what exactly a bookmark is."
---

Bookmarks are a derived of annotations, usually without a text selection, highlights or note. The implementation of what exactly is a bookmark is left at the discretion of the developer but at its core, it can be built upon annotations. Visit the [Annotations](/learn/annotations) section to learn more.
